Welcome to the HChat documentation.

HChat provides real-time multi-user chat rooms with zero friction to join.

## Create a Room

1. Go to https://chat.hn3.top
2. Click **New Room** and pick a name
3. Share the invite link with your friends

## Developer Notes

Rooms are powered by WebSocket for real-time message sync. The identity system supports anonymous join or nickname-based profiles.
