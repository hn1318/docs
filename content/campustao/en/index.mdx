Welcome to the CampusTao documentation.

CampusTao is a campus second-hand book marketplace organized by school, course and major.

## Sell a Book

1. Sign in and choose your school
2. Take a photo and set a price
3. Publish — it appears in the matching course/major feed

## Buy a Book

- Browse by school, course or major
- Chat with the seller directly in-app
- Meet on campus for pickup
